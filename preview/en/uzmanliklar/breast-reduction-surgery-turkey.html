Breast Reduction Surgery Turkey

Breast reduction surgery Turkey is performed for purposes such as removing excess fat and skin tissue from the breasts and eliminating problems arising from large breasts such as back pain. There is a misconception among the public that big breasts always create an attractive or more feminine appearance. However, what makes breasts attractive most of the time is that they have a structure that is compatible with each other and body measurements such as shoulder width. Some individuals may have breasts that are much larger than what is considered ideal. This causes some problems, both aesthetic and functional. Breast reduction aesthetics is performed to eliminate these problems. In the rest of our article, we will be answering FAQs such as “Why is breast reduction performed?”, “How long does breast reduction surgery take?”, “Who can undergo breast reduction surgery?” and “Can breastfeeding be performed after breast reduction?”.

Frequently Asked Questions

Breast reduction is an operation performed to reduce the size of the breasts in line with the individual’s demands and aesthetic criterias, and to eliminate problems such as sagging and lower back pain that occur due to large breasts. Thanks to this operation, the individual can have attractive breasts that are compatible with their body size. Moreover, it is possible to perform this operation together with breast lift surgery. Thus, the breasts gain an upright and aesthetically pleasing appearance that is compatible with each other and body size.

Breast reduction may be performed for purposes such as:

  • Reducing the breasts so that they are compatible with body measurements such as shoulder width.
  • To eliminate the size difference between breasts.
  • To give the breasts an attractive shape.
  • Eliminating back and neck pain caused by large breasts.

Breast reduction aesthetics can be performed due to the aforementioned reasons.

Plastic surgery operations such as breast reduction surgery are only performed on individuals who are of legal age. Individuals who are of legal age and have completed breast development can have breast reduction whenever they want, upon request. However, it is recommended that women who are pregnant or breastfeeding have this operation after the mentioned processes are completed. Likewise, if the individual plans to become pregnant in the near future, it is recommended to have breast augmentation surgery after pregnancy, birth and breastfeeding.

It is possible for women who have had breast reduction to breastfeed their babies after the recovery process is completed. Contrary to popular belief, important tissues that make breastfeeding possible, such as mammary glands and milk ducts, are not damaged during breast surgeries such as breast reduction. Therefore, individuals who have had breast reduction can breastfeed their babies after giving birth.

Breast lift surgery can be performed together with breast reduction surgery. Thus, breasts can be both reduced and lifted in a single operation.

Breast reduction surgery carries some risks, like all other surgical operations. However, most of these risks can be eliminated by choosing the right doctor who carries their surgeries in an ideal operating room environment.

The duration of breast reduction surgery may vary depending on the size of the breasts, the method preferred during the operation and similar factors. This operation is usually completed within 2 to 4 hours. Your doctor will prepare a special breast reduction surgery plan for you and will inform you in detail about how long the operation will take.

Breast reduction surgery Turkey can be performed under general or local anaesthesia. The operation is performed under sterile operating room conditions, within the scope of a personalized surgery plan prepared by the doctor. Special techniques such as “Inverted T” or “Lollipop Incision” may be used during the operation. Thanks to these techniques, excess fat and skin tissue from the breasts are removed. The incisions used during the procedure are closed with special aesthetic stitching procedures and the operation ends. Individuals do not feel pain or discomfort during the operation.

After breast reduction surgery Turkey, it is possible for the individual to be discharged on the same day or to be hospitalized overnight. Patients who are discharged are advised to take a break from their daily routines and not engage in heavy or strenuous activities for about a week. Afterwards, you can return to daily life in accordance with the doctor’s recommendation.

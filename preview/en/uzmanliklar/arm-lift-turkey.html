Arm Lift Turkey (Brachioplasty Surgery)

Arm lift Turkey is an advanced surgical operation performed to tighten and slim the arms. In particular, sagging may occur in the arms due to factors such as rapid weight gain and loss, pregnancy and advancing age. These sagging can negatively affect the appearance of both the arms and the overall appearance of the body. Arm lift surgery is performed to eliminate these sagging, tighten the arms and eliminate the disharmony between the arms and the rest of the body. Thus, individuals can have sporty and attractive arms that are compatible with their bodies. This operation is generally preferred after procedures such as obesity/bariatric surgery and pregnancy. In the rest of our article, we will be answering FAQs such as “How is arm lift surgery performed?”, “How is arm lift surgery performed?”, “How long does arm lift surgery last?” and “Is arm lift surgery permanent?”.

For detailed information about arm lift surgery and similar operations or treatments in Turkey, you can contact Dr. Alper Burak Uslu (MD, FEBOPRAS).

What is Arm Lift Surgery aka Brachioplasty?

Arm lift surgery is an operation performed to eliminate aesthetic problems in the area between the wrist and shoulders, eliminate the appearance of flabby arms(also known as bat wings) and eliminate incompatibilities between the arms. It is possible to use various surgical techniques and special applications such as liposuction during this operation. Arm lift Turkey is an operation that can be performed on both arms. Thanks to this operation, the arms can be thinned and the symmetry problems between the arms can be carefully eliminated.

Why is Arm Lift Performed?

Arm lift surgery can be performed for reasons such as:

  • To eliminate sagging and stretch marks in the arms.
  • To ensure that the arms have a harmonious appearance with each other.
  • To ensure that the arms have aesthetic integrity with the rest of the body.
  • To slim down the arms and add an aesthetic dimension to the arms.
  • Creating a tight and attractive arm appearance.

Arm lift surgery can be performed for the aforementioned purposes.

Who Can Have Arm Lift?

Individuals who want to have arm lift surgery must be of legal age. Because during this operation, surgical procedures are performed for purely aesthetic purposes. Individuals whose arm tissues have completed their development and who are underage cannot therefore have arm lift surgery. Individuals over the age of 18 can have arm lift surgery whenever they want, unless they have a disease or an underlying condition that may prevent the operation to take place.

Is Arm Lift Surgery Risky?

Arm lift surgery carries some risks such as infection and bleeding. However, most of these risks arise because the operation is not performed under ideal operating room conditions and by an expert team. Therefore, choosing a doctor for arm lift surgery is extremely important.

Is Arm Lift Permanent?

The results achieved with arm lift surgery are maintained throughout life if a healthy eating habit and an ideal exercise routine are maintained. However, individuals who do not pay attention to their diet, do not exercise or engage in physical activity, and do not change their lifestyle may lose these results that had been obtained via brachioplasty over the years due to reasons such as weight gain.

How Long Does Arm Lift Turkey Take?

Arm lift Turkey may take up to 2 to 3 hours, depending on the techniques used during the surgery and the procedures performed on the arms. Your doctor will inform you in detail about the estimated duration of the surgery and similar issues.

How is Arm Lift Surgery Performed?

Various surgical procedures and applications such as liposuction may be used during arm lift surgery. These applications are mostly performed under general anaesthesia. Thus, patients do not feel pain during the operation and the operation can be performed ideally. During the surgery, the doctor may perform procedures such as arm thinning, arm stretching and arm tightening. These procedures are performed by removing excess fat and skin tissues from the arms. Procedures such as liposuction or vaser-liposuction can also be used to remove fat tissues. To get detailed information about the subject, you can review the articles titled Vaser-Liposuction and Liposuction on our website.

What is the Recovery Process After Arm Lift Turkey?

Individuals who have arm lift surgery can be discharged on the same day or stay in the hospital for one night, depending on the extent of the surgery they have undergone and their post-surgical state/wellbeing. Discharged individuals can gradually return to their daily lives after 2 to 3 weeks. You should not take a shower for the first two days after arm lift surgery and no movements that will strain your arms should be made until full recovery occurs.

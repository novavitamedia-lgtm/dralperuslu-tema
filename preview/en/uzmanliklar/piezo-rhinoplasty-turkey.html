Piezo Rhinoplasty Turkey

Piezo rhinoplasty Turkey is an advanced technique used in rhinoplasty surgeries. In rhinoplasty surgeries with traditional/classical approaches, physical force is used to shape tissues such as the nasal bone and eliminate deformities in these tissues. This results in the surrounding tissues being affected to an extent by these procedures, resulting in bruising and swelling around the nose after surgery. In the piezo rhinoplasty technique, concentrated sound/Ultrasonic waves are used to shape the tissues in question. Thus, only target tissues are intervened. Thanks to this application, it is possible to perform much more gentle procedures on the nose. Frequently Asked Questions

Piezo rhinoplasty is a modern and advantageous nose aesthetic operation in many aspects, allowing the nasal tissues to be shaped with ultrasonic sound waves. Thanks to this operation, there is no need to use physical force while shaping the nasal bone. In this way, other nasal tissues or tissues around the nose are not worn or damaged during the surgery. With Piezo rhinoplasty, crookedness, humps, bumps and deformities in the nasal bone can be carefully eliminated. The shaping of the nose can be carried out extremely precisely. This special rhinoplasty technique is preferred by more and more patients and surgeons every day.

Piezo rhinoplasty can be performed for purposes such as:

  • To eliminate problems related to the nasal bone without damaging the soft tissues around the nose.
  • Eliminating the appearance of the hump on the nose.
  • To create an aesthetic harmony between the face and nose.
  • Correcting nasal bone deformities.
  • Lifting the tip of the nose.
  • Reducing the nose to be more compatible with the face.

Piezo rhinoplasty can be performed for the reasons mentioned.

Piezo rhinoplasty operation can be performed on individuals who want to have nose surgery but are afraid of post-operative bruises and swelling. It is normal to see a certain amount of swelling and bruising after this operation as well, however, these are fewer than the complications encountered after rhinoplasty operations with traditional approach and they disappear much more quickly. Any individual over the age of 18 can have piezo rhinoplasty upon request. This operation can be performed on both men and women.

The advantages of piezo rhinoplasty include:

  • This operation is performed with extreme precision.
  • Procedures based on physical force are not performed during the operation. Therefore, other tissues are not damaged.
  • Since soft tissues are not damaged, complaints such as bruising and swelling are less common.
  • The healing process is much more comfortable and faster.

The advantages of piezo rhinoplasty can be listed as above.

Before piezo rhinoplasty surgery, the patient is anaesthetized. The type and details of this anaesthesia procedure are determined according to the scope of the operation. The patient does not feel pain or discomfort during the operation. Thus, the operation is performed safely and ideally.

Piezo rhinoplasty Turkey can take up to 4 to 5 hours. However, some small-scale operations can be completed in a relatively short time, such as 2 hours. Your doctor will give detailed information about the duration of piezo rhinoplasty and similar issues before the surgery.

Although piezo rhinoplasty resembles the traditional rhinoplasty in some aspects, it has many different aspects from the traditional approach. During this operation, the patient is first anaesthetized. Then, the targeted nasal tissues are reached with the help of minimal incisions. Then, tissues such as the nasal bone are shaped with the help of a special device that emits ultrasonic sound waves, instead of cutting tools. Unlike the operations with traditional approach, physical force is not used, thus, damage and wear of the surrounding tissues is prevented. After the planned procedures are completed, the minimal incisions are carefully stitched and the surgery ends.

After piezo rhinoplasty, patients can be discharged on the same day or stay in the hospital for a day. After the operation, as we mentioned before, bruising and swelling are seen in a minimal extent. However, it is normal to see some bruising. The oedema will largely dissipate in approximately 1 to 2 weeks. During this process, the recommendations given by the doctor should be followed carefully and the prescribed medications should be used regularly.

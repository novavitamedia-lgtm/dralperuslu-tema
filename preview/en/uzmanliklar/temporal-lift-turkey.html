Temporal Lift Turkey

Temporal lift Turkey, also known as temporal eyebrow lift, is performed to give the upper part of the face an attractive appearance. Tissues in the temple area may lose their ideal position and sag over time, as a result, drooping of the eyebrows may occur. Some individuals have this appearance due to genetic reasons. This appearance can be eliminated during Temporal Lift surgery, in this way, an ideal appearance can be given to the eyebrows and problems such as wrinkles around the eyebrows and forehead can be eliminated. In the remaining of our article, we will be answering FAQs such as “How is temporal lift surgery performed?”, “How long does temporal lift take?”, “To whom can temporal lift be performed?” and “Is temporal lift painful?”.

Frequently Asked Questions

Temple lift surgery is a special operation performed to eliminate sagging in the temple area, eliminate crow’s feet wrinkles and give the eyebrows an ideal position. Thanks to this operation, aesthetic problems in the upper part of the face can be carefully eliminated. Thus, the aesthetic integrity of the face can be reinforced. Ultimately, an extremely attractive and youthful facial appearance is created. This operation is also known by names such as eyebrow lift.

Temple area; It is a hollow area between the ear, cheek, forehead and eye. The temples on both sides of the head may develop various aesthetic problems over time. These problems can be eliminated through operations known as temporal lift or temporal eyebrow lift.

Temporal lift can be performed for the following reasons:

  • To eliminate wrinkles and sagging in the temple area.
  • To ensure that the forehead area has a smooth appearance.
  • To give the eyebrows an ideal position.
  • To eliminate crow’s feet/wrinkles around the eyes.
  • Tightening the eye area.

Temporal lift surgery can be performed for the aforementioned purposes.

Temporal lift surgery can be performed on any individual who is of legal age. This application is becoming more and more preferred every day both in the world and in our country. Nowadays, both women and men frequently consult plastic surgeons for a temporal lift surgery. However, temporal lift surgery is not recommended for individuals under 18 years of age. Minors must wait until they are 18 years old to have this operation.

Temple lift is not a painful operation. During this operation, the patient is given general anaesthesia. Thus, the patient can be prevented from feeling pain due to the procedures performed during the surgery. Anaesthesia applied during temporal lift surgery is planned by the doctor and anaesthesiologist specifically for the details of the surgery to be performed.

Temple lift surgery takes approximately 2 to 4 hours. In some cases, it is normal for the surgery to take up to 6 hours. Your doctor will provide detailed information about the estimated duration of temporal lifting surgery before the surgery.

Before temporal lift surgery, a detailed interview is first held with the patient. During this meeting, the patient’s expectations from the surgery and the appearance they want to achieve are listened to. Then, various examinations are performed on the temple area. Ultimately, a surgery plan is made that meets the individual’s demands and aesthetic criterias.

When it comes to the surgery phase, the first procedure used will be general anaesthesia. Thanks to general anaesthesia, the patient is completely prevented from feeling pain and discomfort. During the procedures, the tissues in the temple area are rearranged and the targeted appearance is created. Thus, the surgery is completed.

Individuals who have had a Temporal Lift surgery usually stay in the hospital for 1 night. The patients get discharged with special dressings and a bandage. The dressing is usually removed within 2 days but the bandage must be worn by the patients for up to 2 weeks. Patients in the recovery process are advised to sleep on their back for a while, avoid touching the temple area, and use the medications prescribed by the doctor regularly.

Tummy Tuck Turkey

Tummy tuck Turkey; is a special operation performed to create a slim and tight abdominal appearance. Thanks to this operation, individuals can get rid of sagging, cracks and lines in their abdominal areas. Abdominoplasty surgery is frequently preferred, especially after the processes such as bariatric surgery and post-pregnancy. In the rest of our article, we will be answering FAQs such as “How is mini tummy tuck surgery performed?”, “How long does tummy tuck surgery take?”, “Who can have tummy tuck surgery?” and “What is the recovery process like after tummy tuck surgery?”.

For detailed information about Turkey abdominoplasty and mini-abdominoplasty surgery, or similar operations/treatments you can contact Dr. Alper Burak Uslu (MD, FEBOPRAS).

What is Abdominoplasty aka Tummy-Tuck?

Abdominoplasty is an operation performed using special surgical techniques to eliminate sagging and stretch marks in the abdominal area. Thanks to this operation, aesthetic problems such as sagging in the abdominal area can be eliminated and a tight abdominal appearance can be achieved. During tummy tuck aesthetics, advanced procedures and surgical procedures such as liposuction are used. Thanks to these applications, an aesthetic integrity is created between the waist, abdomen and navel area. The tummy tuck aesthetic is planned by the doctor who performs the operation, completely in accordance with the individual’s demands and general aesthetic criterias.

What is Mini Tummy Tuck?

Mini tummy tuck is a smaller-scale aesthetic operation compared to the classic tummy tuck surgery. Procedures such as belly button positioning are generally not performed during this operation, and much more regional/small-scale operations are used. Therefore, the recovery period after mini tummy tuck is much shorter than that of a full tummy tuck.

Why is Tummy Tuck Done?

Abdominoplasty surgery can be performed for the following reasons:

  • To eliminate sagging in the abdominal area caused by weight gain and similar factors.
  • Slimming the abdomen and waist.
  • To ensure that the abdomen and waist area gain an aesthetically attractive appearance.
  • To eliminate stretch marks, wrinkles and similar aesthetic problems occurring in the abdominal area.

Abdominal stretching surgery can be performed for the mentioned purposes.

Who Can Have Tummy Tuck Surgery?

Tummy tuck surgery involves extremely comprehensive surgical procedures and applications. Therefore, this operation is not recommended for minors. However, individuals who have completed tissue development in the abdominal area and are over the age of 18 can have abdominoplasty surgery whenever they want. Individuals who are planning to become pregnant soon or are already pregnant are advised to have abdominoplasty surgery later on. The results achieved with tummy tuck surgery are usually not affected by the body changes that occur during pregnancy and are much more permanent.

Is Tummy Tuck Turkey Dangerous?

Abdominoplasty is not considered to be a risky operation. However, there are some factors that can make this operation dangerous and risky. These factors often arise because the operation is performed by a non-expert team under non-ideal operating room conditions. Therefore, choosing a doctor for abdominoplasty is extremely important.

Is Tummy Tuck Permanent?

Individuals who want to preserve the outcomes achieved with tummy tuck surgery for a lifetime are advised to exercise regularly after the surgery. Likewise, they are advised to regulate their eating habits and adopt a healthy lifestyle.

How Long Does a Tummy Tuck Take?

As we mentioned before, tummy tuck surgery is performed in two different ways. Total tummy tuck surgery takes approximately 3 to 4 hours. Mini tummy tuck surgery is usually completed within 1 to 2 hours. These periods may vary from person to person depending on the scope of the procedures performed during the surgery, your doctor will provide detailed information about the duration before the surgery during the consultation.

How is a Tummy Tuck Turkey Done?

General anaesthesia is used to prevent the patient from feeling pain during abdominoplasty and to ensure that the surgery is performed ideally. During the surgery, excess fat and skin tissues in the abdominal area are removed using special techniques. The remaining tissues are rearranged to create a tight abdominal appearance. In this process, the position of tissues such as the belly button can also be arranged in line with the purpose we mentioned. During the full tummy tuck surgery, the entire abdomen can be treated, and during the mini tummy tuck surgery, only the lower part of the abdomen can be treated.

What is the Recovery Process After Tummy Tuck?

Those who have abdominoplasty will have a planned stay in the hospital for 1 or 2 nights after the surgery. They are then discharged and allowed to return home. After abdominoplasty, movements that will force the abdominal area should not be made to avoid damaging the surgical area. You should rest in bed for at least a week, and only perform activities permitted by your doctor. Patients use a special corset during the recovery period after abdominoplasty. Thanks to this corset, the healing process will be more ideal and faster. It is possible to return to work 2 to 4 weeks after surgery, but some restrictions must be followed until full recovery occurs.
